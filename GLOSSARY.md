# Patch Helper glossary

## Language

**AUX bus**:
An internal disting NT signal bus, identified as AUX 1 through AUX 44.
_Avoid_: AUX socket, expander output

**AUX row**:
A record describing what one selected AUX bus carries, with Description and Group fields.

**Description**:
The text explaining the purpose or content of an AUX bus.

**Group**:
A user-defined label that associates related patch records.
